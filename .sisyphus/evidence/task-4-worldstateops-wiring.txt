    RP_AGENT_FRAMEWORK_INSTRUCTIONS body.

    the Talker allowed-output sentence and appends a Talker-mode
    "World State Ops" section.
  - When disabled, NEITHER block is injected.
  - actionCommitments instructions remain untouched.
