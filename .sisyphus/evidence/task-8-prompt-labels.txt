  renderTypedRetrieval() now prepends [basis=X provenance=Y verification=Z] on weak segments.

## Weak-label rule
Applied when ANY of:
1. basis is belief or unknown
2. basis is inference AND verification is NOT strong_verified
3. verification is unverified (regardless of basis)

NOT applied when basis is first_hand/hearsay/introspection AND verification is context_verified or strong_verified.
